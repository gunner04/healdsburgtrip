§ 03 · The Kitchen

The meal plan

Flavor arc: Greek Thursday → Italian Friday → Mediterranean board Saturday lunch → Mediterranean lamb feast Saturday dinner → smoked salmon picnic Sunday.

THURSDAY DINNER · BBQ #1

Greek lemon-oregano chicken

Plate: Chicken thighs + grilled veg + Greek village salad + tzatziki + pita + grilled lemons.

Chicken marinade (mix in Ziploc at Airbnb ~5:45 p.m., 60+ min marinade): 1/3 cup olive oil, 1/4 cup lemon juice + zest of 1 lemon, 6 cloves garlic minced, 2 tbsp dried oregano, 1 tbsp salt, 2 tsp black pepper, 1 tsp red pepper flakes, 2 tsp dried thyme. Grill 4–5 min direct each side, then 3–5 min indirect to 165°F. Rest 5 min.

Tzatziki: 1.5 cups Greek yogurt + 1 grated English cucumber (squeezed dry!) + 2 garlic cloves + 1 tbsp olive oil + 1 tbsp lemon + 1/2 tsp salt + 2 tbsp chopped dill.

Greek village salad: 3 tomatoes chunked + 1 cucumber sliced + 1/2 red onion thin + 1 cup Kalamata olives + 6 oz feta in slabs (not crumbled) + dressing of 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tsp oregano, S&P. No lettuce.

Grilled veg: Zucchinis (1/2-in planks), shishitos in basket, red onion rings, asparagus. Olive oil + salt before grilling. Finish with lemon squeeze.

Grilled lemons: 3 lemons halved, cut-side oiled, grilled cut-side-down 3–4 min. One half per plate to squeeze.

Kian: Set aside one plain chicken thigh before marinating. Plain zucchini, torn pita, cucumber + feta crumble.

FRIDAY DINNER · LIGHT

Pasta + sheet-pan shrimp

Plate: Aglio e olio + sheet-pan lemon-garlic shrimp + leftover Thursday grilled veg + simple salad.

Aglio e olio (25 min): 1 lb spaghetti or bucatini cooked al dente. Sauté 6 sliced garlic cloves in 1/4 cup olive oil low + slow until pale gold (3–4 min). Add 1 tsp red pepper flakes. Toss with pasta + 1/2 cup reserved pasta water. Finish with chopped parsley + parmesan + black pepper.

Sheet-pan lemon-garlic shrimp (15 min, parallel to pasta): Oven to 400°F. Toss 1.5 lbs peeled deveined shrimp (thawed if frozen) with 3 tbsp olive oil, 4 cloves garlic minced, zest + juice of 1 lemon, 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes, 1/2 tsp black pepper, 1 tbsp chopped parsley. Spread on sheet pan. Roast 8–10 min until pink. Don't overcook — shrimp turn rubbery fast. Fresh lemon squeeze before serving.

Side salad: Mixed greens + lemon-dijon vinaigrette (3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp lemon, 1 tsp dijon, S&P).

Kian: Plain shrimp pieces (chopped small), plain pasta with butter + parm.

SATURDAY LUNCH · NO COOKING

The market board

Build a board, don't cook. Spread on a big platter:

Cheeses (2–3 varieties, room temp 30 min before serving), sliced bread + crackers, salami/cured meat, sliced strawberries + cherries, sliced cucumber + snap peas + radishes (raw), hummus + olive oil + za'atar, marinated olives, hard-boiled eggs cut in half with flaky salt, honey + jam + butter, fresh herbs scattered.

From Thursday Big Johns shop: hummus, olives, feta, sourdough, eggs (hard-boil Fri night). From Saturday farmers' market: cheese, fruit, salami, additional bread, honey, tomatoes, snap peas.

SATURDAY DINNER · BBQ #2 / FEAST

Mediterranean lamb feast

Plate: Butterflied leg of lamb + chimichurri + grilled peach + burrata + elote corn + market salad + garlic bread + pita.

Lamb marinade (apply 12:30–1 p.m., let sit 4–6 hrs): 1/2 cup olive oil, 6 garlic minced, 3 tbsp lemon juice + zest of 2 lemons, 3 tbsp fresh rosemary chopped, 2 tbsp fresh thyme, 2 tbsp Dijon, 1 tbsp salt, 1.5 tsp black pepper. Optional: 1 tsp cumin + 1 tsp coriander.

Lamb method: Room temp 30 min before grilling. Sear direct high heat 4–5 min/side. Move to indirect, close lid, cook to 130°F internal (medium-rare), ~20–25 min more. Rest 15 min minimum. Slice thin, against the grain.

Chimichurri: 1 large bunch parsley + 4 garlic cloves + 1/4 cup red wine vinegar + 1/2 cup olive oil + 1 tsp dried oregano + 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes + 1 tsp salt. Pulse in food processor.

Grilled peach + burrata: 4 ripe peaches halved + pitted, cut-side oiled, grilled cut-side-down 3–4 min. Plate with torn burrata, olive oil drizzle, balsamic glaze, flaky salt, torn basil. The showstopper. 10 min.

Elote corn: 4 ears husked, grilled direct heat 8–10 min turning every 2 min. Brush with mayo + sour cream + lime juice + chili powder + cotija/feta + cilantro.

Garlic bread: Sourdough sliced thick, slathered with garlic-herb butter (1 stick butter + 4 garlic cloves minced + 1/4 cup parsley + 1/2 tsp salt). Foil-wrapped, indirect grill side 10 min while lamb rests.

Kian: Sliced lamb (well-done end pieces) no chimichurri. Corn kernels off cob. Plain bread.

§ 06 · Risks

Flags & gotchas

  • Hertz pickup decoupled from Healdsburg run. You + wife do the SFO run Thursday morning, drive both cars home, park rental, install car seat at home. Family departure for Healdsburg is 1:30 p.m.
  • Two-car convoy throughout. Personal car carries you, bike, possibly Kian + wife. Rental carries the rest. Use phone navigation independently — don't try to literally follow each other.
  • Sunday Hertz return is also decoupled. Unload at home first. Drop the rental later. Confirm contract deadline. Hertz SFO is 24/7.
  • Sunday checkout is 10 a.m. — pack starts the night before. Pack non-essentials Saturday night so Sunday morning is clean.
  • Champions League final = exact 9:00 a.m. PT kickoff. Arsenal vs PSG. Saturday bike must be done by 9 a.m. sharp. Plan for extra time + pens pushing end to ~11:45 a.m.
  • Samnzar is a one-man, weekends-only operation. Greg Brummett runs it solo. Articles from 2023 flagged he was struggling. Call 707-829-6699 Saturday morning to confirm Sunday hours.
  • Wild Flour Sunday line can be 20+ min. Walk-up only, no inside seating. Split duty: one adult queues, others wait in garden with Kian.
  • Coastal weather is 10–15°F cooler than Healdsburg + windy. If picking Doran: pack jackets, beanie for Kian, blanket. Default picnic (Ragle Ranch) is inland and avoids this.
  • Thursday 1:30 p.m. departure may hit traffic. 101 N weekend-eve traffic builds after 2 p.m. Leaving at 1:00 is safer.
  • Lamb marinade timing: 4–6 hours. Apply Saturday 12:30–1 p.m. for 6 p.m. grilling. Take out of fridge 30 min before grilling for room-temp meat.
  • Berry season timing. Late May is early for Sonoma raspberries; strawberries more likely. Farmers' market hedges this.
